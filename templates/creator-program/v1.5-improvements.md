## 2. v1.5 improvements (priority-ordered, with metrics)

### 2.1 Shorten the intake form (P0 — highest ROI)

**Observed:** 6 of 30 (20%) drop off mid-intake. Current form has 14 fields.

**Change:** cut to 6 mandatory fields — handle, niche, follower count, chosen template, primary content goal, "is this you in 1 sentence". Move the rest to a second-pass optional follow-up after the first manifest is delivered.

**Metric:** drop-off ≤ 8% (≤ 1 in 30) within v1.5's first 30 sign-ups.

**Files:** `templates/creator-program/intake-form.md` (new — short version) + DM template tweak.

### 2.2 Pre-built "Tuning Kits" for the top 5 templates (P0)

**Observed:** the slowest step is manifest tuning (avg 22h of the 36h delivery window). Top 5 templates account for 25 / 30 (83%) of requests.

**Change:** ship pre-tuned manifest + system-prompt drafts for the top 5 templates per top-3 niches (15 kits total). On delivery, the operator only needs to hand-tune voice + bridges — the rest is template-driven.

**Metric:** avg DM-to-delivery time ≤ 18h (cut in half).

**Files:** `templates/creator-program/tuning-kits/<niche>/<template>.yaml` (15 files, future — not in this prompt).

### 2.3 Auto-disclaimer DM macro for monetization-optimizer (P0)

**Observed:** 7 of 30 (23%) requested `monetization-optimizer`. The V.1+V.2+Vietnam-resident disclaimer text is non-negotiable but slows DM throughput.

**Change:** ship a one-keystroke DM macro (`disclaimers/monetization-optimizer.md`) with the exact V.1+V.2+Vietnam addendum text. The `outreach-tracker.py deliver` command already prints the reminder; the macro lets the operator paste in three seconds.

**Metric:** 0 monetization-optimizer deliveries shipped without the V.1+V.2 footer (current target). Verified by spot-checking via `creator-dashboard.py`.

**Files:** `templates/creator-program/disclaimers/monetization-optimizer.md` (new, future).

### 2.4 Niche → recommended-template suggestions in the dashboard (P1)

**Observed:** creators frequently change template choice mid-conversation when shown what other creators in their niche picked.

**Change:** `creator-dashboard.py` shows a "Niches × Templates" matrix derived from the local outreach + testimonials store. The operator can paste this into a DM ("Here's what creators in fintech are picking — `monetization-optimizer` is the top, but `analytics-summarizer` pairs well via the bridge"). This is built in this prompt.

**Metric:** ≥ 30% of intake DMs include a niche-tailored suggestion within v1.5's first 30 sign-ups.

**Files:** `templates/creator-program/creator-dashboard.py` (this prompt).

### 2.5 Surface the 11 zero-signup templates in DM macros (P1)

**Observed:** 11 of the 20 templates got zero sign-ups in the first window. These aren't worse — they're less discoverable.

**Change:** in the welcome DM, list all 20 templates grouped per the launch-thread tweets 3-6 (Content / Engagement / Analytics / Audience+Brand+Money) AND mention 1-2 of the under-loved templates that pair well with the creator's stated niche. Examples:

- ai-agents niche → suggest `competitor-watch` + `niche-influencer-finder`
- fintech niche → suggest `brand-voice-trainer` + `cross-platform-reposter`
- fitness niche → suggest `content-calendar-builder` + `comment-engagement-booster`

**Metric:** sign-ups distributed across ≥ 14 templates by the end of v1.5's first 30 (vs 9 in v1.0).

**Files:** `templates/creator-program/dm-macros/welcome.md` (new, future).

### 2.6 Weekly testimonials → public X thread (P2)

**Observed:** with `testimonial-collector.py` shipped (P105), the operator can publish a 5-card X thread weekly without breaking consent rules.

**Change:** add a `weekly-testimonial-thread.py` that reads `testimonials.json` and emits an 8-tweet thread (1 hook + up to 6 cards + 1 CTA), preserving the V.1+V.2 footer for any monetization-optimizer card.

**Metric:** 1 thread / week, ≥ 6 testimonial cards across each thread, 0 consent-violation incidents.

**Files:** `templates/creator-program/weekly-testimonial-thread.py` (future).

### 2.7 Auto-stale flagging in outreach-tracker (P2)

**Observed:** 4 of 30 (13%) entries sit in `pending` >7 days; the weekly report flags them but the operator doesn't always action them.

**Change:** add a `mark-stale-after` config (default 7 days) and a `outreach-tracker.py auto-stale` command that flips eligible entries from `pending` to `stale` automatically. Already-`stale` entries still show in the weekly report's stale section.

**Metric:** ≤ 5% of all entries in `pending` >14 days.

**Files:** patch `outreach-tracker.py` in v1.5 patch release.

## 3. What we are NOT changing

- **The 20-template catalog.** It stays at 20. Adding more dilutes; cutting any erases real work.
- **The privacy contract.** Inbound consent only, dual consent flags on testimonials, AppData-only storage. Non-negotiable.
- **The 10k follower floor.** Mid-tier creators are the ICP; sub-10k creators are welcome to use the templates self-serve from the public repo, but bespoke onboarding stays gated.
- **Apache 2.0.** Forever.
- **The Constitution.** Articles I–IX inherit unchanged.
